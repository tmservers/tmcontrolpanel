# Privacy

Last updated: 10/11/2026

This notice explains how TMControlPanel handles personal information when you use TMControlPanel or play on the Trackmania servers it manages. TMControlPanel is self-hosted software; the operator of this installation determines how it is configured and how information is used.

For privacy questions or requests, contact marijn.regterschot@gmail.com.

## Information we process

Depending on the features you use, we process:

- **Account information:** your Trackmania account identifier, login, display name, Ubisoft identifier where available, and assigned roles, permissions, and group memberships. Sign-in takes place through Nadeo; you do not enter your Ubisoft password into this panel.
- **Player and match information:** player logins and nicknames, in-game location information where supplied by the server, race times, checkpoints, scores, match results, and player moderation information. Gameplay information can be processed even if you do not sign in to the panel.
- **Content and administration activity:** files and settings you submit, notifications, and audit records of management actions, including the account involved, action, time, and relevant details.
- **Technical information:** connection information, error reports, and service logs. Our hosting and network services may also process IP addresses, request details, and browser information.

## How we use information

We use this information to sign you in, manage access, operate Trackmania servers, display live game information and recorded results, carry out requested actions, investigate errors, and maintain service security.

## Cookies and browser storage

The panel uses cookies to support sign-in and session security. It also uses browser storage or cookies to remember interface preferences, such as the theme and sidebar state. Blocking or clearing this storage may sign you out or reset preferences.

## Optional features and external services

The following features depend on this installation's configuration:

- **Analytics:** if enabled, Plausible receives page-view and related usage information. Page addresses sent for analytics may include query parameters.
- **Error monitoring:** if enabled, Sentry receives diagnostic information and, depending on configuration, performance traces, application logs, and session replays.
- **AI assistant:** if Codriver is enabled and used, requests and relevant game or server context are sent to the configured AI services. The panel can store request text, replies, tool actions, feedback, and usage information.
- **Integrations and plugins:** Nadeo, Trackmania Exchange, Hetzner, result-submission services, and installed plugins may process information needed to perform enabled functions. Plugin access depends on the permissions granted by administrators.

## Who can access information

Panel administrators and other authorized users can access information according to their permissions. Player names and results may also be visible in-game or through public groups and shared records, depending on server and panel settings. Hosting providers and enabled external services process information needed to deliver their functions.

## How long information is kept

Disabling an account or removing an item from view does not necessarily erase its underlying records. Some records are marked as deleted and remain stored until separately removed. Backups may retain earlier copies according to 30 days.

## Your choices and requests

Contact marijn.regterschot@gmail.com to ask about your information or request access, correction, or deletion. Depending on the law that applies, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy of your information, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority.

Include your Trackmania login and the relevant server or panel so we can locate your records. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Please do not send passwords or API keys.

## Changes to this notice

We will update this page when our privacy practices change and revise the date above.
